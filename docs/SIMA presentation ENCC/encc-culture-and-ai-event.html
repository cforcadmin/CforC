Culture and AI: shaping alternative narratives, finding a common voice

⊙ Online ▣ 28 May 2025 ⏲ 10:30 – 12:00 CET

Share your experiences with AI and have your say on the European Commission’s AI strategy at the first 2025 meeting of our Digital Ethics working group.

Our work on Artificial Intelligence continues. On May 28th, from 10:30 until 12:00, join the upcoming gathering of our Digital Ethics working group. On the agenda:

  1. 1

    We will share our experiences, doubts, frustrations, and feelings about so-called Artificial Intelligence and its use in cultural work. These inputs will then feature, in the form of short stories, in our upcoming dedicated publication.

  2. 2

    The European Commission is currently drafting a strategy that aims to push developments in AI and its capillary adoption by industry as well as the public sector. We will discuss this and a possible common statement to publish as part of the related call for vidence (i.e., public consultation).

N.B. No specific knowledge on digital is needed to join our meetings, just a genuine interest in the topic.
