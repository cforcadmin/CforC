Hosted by Culture Action Europe, this meeting was a great opportunity to discuss our project to move away from Big-Tech, extractive digital tools, and towards infrastructures whose values align with those of socio-cultural organisations.

Participants Who joins

Our working group on Digital Ethics is a transectorial platform open to ENCC members, other cultural operators, partner networks, civil society organisations, and students and educators from the IT sector.

Meetings of the group have been ongoing since August 2021. Experts and practitioners with experience, needs, challenges, or questions to share with the group are welcome to join.

  • ENCC members
  • Other cultural operators
  • Partner networks
  • Civil society organisations
  • Students & educators from the IT sector
